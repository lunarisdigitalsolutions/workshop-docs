Quick fixes for common problems when ordering or working the kitchen board.

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "We don't deliver to this postal code" | Your address is outside all delivery zones. Choose **Pickup** instead. |
| No confirmation email | Check your spam folder. The email comes from `orders@pizzaluna.example`. |
| Order stays **Placed** for more than five minutes | Do not order again. Call the branch with your order number. |
| Kitchen board shows no orders | Check that the tablet is online and that you are signed in to the right branch. |
