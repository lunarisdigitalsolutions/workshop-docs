In five minutes you will order a pizza for delivery and receive a tracking link. You do not need an
account.

## 1. Choose how you get your pizza

Open the shop and select **Delivery** or **Pickup**.

- **Delivery:** enter your postal code. The shop picks the branch that delivers to you.
- **Pickup:** choose a branch. The Campus branch offers pickup only. You recieve a reminder when
  your order is ready, and you show this code at the counter:

```
OT-0815
```

## 2. Build your order

1. Pick a pizza, for example **Margerita**, and choose **Regular** (26 cm) or **Large** (32 cm).
2. Add up to five extra toppings.
3. Select **Add to cart**. Repeat for drinks and sides.

For delivery, the order needs a value of at least 15.00 €. Delivery is free from 30.00 €.

## 3. Check out

1. Enter your name, email address and – for delivery – your address.
2. Pay by card. Your card details go directly to our payment provider.
3. Select **Place order**.

## 4. Follow your order

You see your order number, for example `OT-0815`, and receive an email with a tracking link. Your order
shows **Placed** for a few seconds, then **Accepted** once the payment is confirmed.
