## Do I need an account to order?

No. You order as a guest and follow your order with the tracking link we send by email.

## Which branch prepares my order?

For delivery, the branch whose delivery zone includes your postal code. For pickup, the branch you
choose.

## Can I change my order after placing it?

No. Cancel it while the status is **Accepted** and place a new one.

## Why can't I order right now?

The branch is closed, closes within the next 30 minutes, or has paused online orders during a rush.
