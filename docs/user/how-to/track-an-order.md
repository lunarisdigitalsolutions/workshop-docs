---
title: How to track an order
sidebar_position: 1
tags: [user, customer, ordering]
---

Follow an order you placed and cancel it while that is still possible.

1. Open the tracking link from your confirmation email.
2. Read the current status:

   | Status | Meaning |
   | --- | --- |
   | Placed | We received your order and are confirming the payment. |
   | Accepted | The branch has your order. You can still cancel it. |
   | In the oven | Your pizza is being prepared. |
   | Ready for pickup | Collect it at the branch counter with your order number. |
   | Out for delivery | Your order is on its way. |
   | Completed | Enjoy your meal. |

3. To cancel, select **Cancel order**. This is possible until the status changes to **In the oven**.
   After that, call the branch.

Something looks wrong? See [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md).
