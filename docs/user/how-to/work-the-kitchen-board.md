For branch staff: handle online orders from acceptance to handover on the kitchen board.

1. Sign in at the branch tablet with your staff account. The board shows your branch's accepted
   orders, oldest first.
2. Select **Start** on the next order. Its status changes to **In the oven** and the customer can no
   longer cancel it.
3. When the order is finished, select **Ready** (pickup) or **Out for delivery** (delivery). The
   customer is notified by email.
4. Select **Complete** when the customer has collected the order or the driver has delivered it.

## Pause online orders

During rush hour, select **Pause orders** and choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes. New orders for your branch
are rejected with a friendly message until the pause ends. Use this when the kitchen cannot finish
new orders within 45 minutes.

## Cancel an order

Branch managers can cancel orders in any status before **Completed**. Orders cancelled after
**In the oven** must be refunded manually by the manager.
