All prices are in euros and include VAT. The menu and its prices are the same in every branch.

## Pizza sizes

| Size | Diameter | Price factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | 26 cm | 1.0 × base price |
| Large | 32 cm | 1.4 × base price, rounded up to the next 0.50 € |

## Toppings

Each extra topping costs 1.50 € on a regular and 2.00 € on a large pizza. A pizza can have at most
five extra toppings.

## Delivery

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum order value for delivery | 15.00 € |
| Delivery fee | 2.50 €, free from 30.00 € order value |
| Pickup | No minimum, no fee |

Discount codes are out of scope for the first release.
